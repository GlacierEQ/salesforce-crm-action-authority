## Bottleneck (GlacierEQ hypothesis)

Trusted autonomous action over CRM context and enterprise integration layers.

**Brick wall:** Preventing incorrect or irreversible actions while maintaining identity, consent, observability, and business semantics.

**Observed public pressure (snapshot hypothesis):** CRM agents are expected to reason and act across customer data, workflows, integrations, and business permissions.

## Innovation mechanism
